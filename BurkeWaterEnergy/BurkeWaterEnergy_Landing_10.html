2029
Burke Closes · Ground Breaks
FAA closure (requires Congressional action and FAA approval — the primary regulatory gate). Runway demolition begins. Site civil work: landfill capping, pile foundation surveys, utility corridors established. Zone 1 water intake system goes in — the foundational infrastructure everything else connects to. Zone 6 grid interconnection substation begins construction. Zone 4 KARNO microgrid first containers arrive on site. Federal H2Hub grant agreement executed.

First Hydrogen Production · 25% Operations
The campus produces hydrogen from Lake Erie water for the first time. 25% of full PEM capacity online. KARNO microgrid transitions from NG to H₂ blend. FastOx gasification commissioned — Cleveland's first MSW delivery arrives. H₂ storage field (Zone 9) first vessels pressurized. Zone 8 marine bunkering pier construction begins at NE corner (Army Corps area now available). Research campus breaks ground. CMSD high school junior technician apprenticeship program launches. LEEDCo Icebreaker Wind construction offshore begins.

2032
Industrial Scale · 60% Operations
PEM farm at 60% capacity — approximately 108 t H₂/day. Haber-Bosch ammonia plant begins production; first green ammonia deliveries to Ohio agriculture market. NH₃ pipeline to NE Ohio industrial corridor under construction. Data center Phase 1 (20 MW) opens for co-location tenants. LEEDCo Icebreaker Wind turbines installed offshore. Research institute buildings occupied; first CWRU PhD cohort begins Burke testbed research. 600+ people employed on campus. KARNO at full 50 MW; 100% H₂ fuel. Grid export revenue begins at scale.

2033
Public Campus Opens · 90% Operations
Lakefront promenade opens to the public — the first time in decades that Clevelanders have free access to this stretch of Lake Erie shoreline. Energy Discovery Centre opens; Great Lakes Science Center partnership activates. Hotel opens. Marine bunkering pier opens for business; first Great Lakes cargo ship takes on green hydrogen. Data center Phase 2 (40 MW) opens. NH₃ pipeline to industrial corridor operational. 900+ campus employees. Army Corps dredge disposal area formally transferred to Port authority for marine use.

2034
Full Operations 🎉
The entire Water-to-Energy Campus is fully operational. 500 MW PEM electrolyser farm producing 180 t H₂/day from Lake Erie water. FastOx converting Cleveland's MSW to zero-landfill syngas. KARNO microgrid delivering 50 MW on 100% green hydrogen. Haber-Bosch synthesising 1,200 t NH₃/day. Data center Phase 3 (60 MW) complete. Marine pier bunkering Great Lakes fleet. Research institute attracting $15M+ in annual DOE and corporate grants. 1,200+ permanent jobs on campus. 5,000-student educational pipeline from CMSD through CWRU operational. Total gross revenue: ~$994M/year including data center, ~$302M/year for energy campus core. The City of Cleveland earns $5M/year in land lease plus $8–12M/year in PILOT payments — forever.
