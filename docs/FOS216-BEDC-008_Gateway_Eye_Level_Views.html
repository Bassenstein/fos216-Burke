01Method

Why these are projected and not drawn

A view drawn by hand agrees with whatever the hand believes. A view projected through a real camera disagrees with the plan whenever the plan is wrong, and it does so loudly — an empty frame, a subject that will not fit, a wall where the sky should be.

This pass caught five things the plans did not show: an aperture design that hid the runway it existed to reveal, a fence post 12 ft in front of a camera, a 28 mm lens that could not hold a 120-ft parapet from 230 ft, a camera position with a building face inside the lens, and a runway that had never been modeled because no earlier view in this set looked south off the property. All five are written up in BEDC-007 §13.

Closing

What to do with these

Each station has a matching photorealistic prompt in FOS216-BEDC-009, keyed by ID, carrying the same lat/lon, heading, lens and eye height plus a computed sun position. Export these eight views as PNGs, attach the AR view first and the BEDC-007 plan second, and the generator has no room to invent geometry.

The three recurring generator failures still apply and are repeated in the negative block: an invented steam plume, glazing that turns the halls into an office building, and the four halls compressed into a single wall.
