Power Architecture

Replacing a substation
with a KARNO microgrid

The KARNO array replaces the conventional lakefront distribution substation with an active, self-generating distributed energy resource that can operate with or without the utility grid entirely. This is a fundamental architectural shift — not an incremental upgrade.

Industrial Chemicals

From trash
and water to
green ammonia

The Haber-Bosch synthesis loop — combining electrolytic hydrogen from Lake Erie water with nitrogen from atmospheric air — produces 1,200 tonnes of zero-carbon ammonia per day. Simultaneously, the FastOx gasifier converts Cleveland's municipal solid waste into syngas that supplements the hydrogen feedstock, creating a genuine circular economy.
