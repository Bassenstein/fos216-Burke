Policy & Federal Strategy

A far more
compelling
federal ask

Every previous Burke proposal asked Congress and the FAA to close an airport for a park. The Water-to-Energy Campus asks for closure to build a nationally significant clean energy infrastructure project. That reframing is the key to unlocking federal funding that dwarfs anything the city has previously accessed.
