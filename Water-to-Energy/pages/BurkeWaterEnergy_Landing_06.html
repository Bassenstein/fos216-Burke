Lake Erie as a
data center
cooling medium

The AI and cloud computing industry is facing a global data center power-cooling crisis: hyperscalers cannot find sites with both reliable low-carbon power and viable cooling solutions as air-cooled facilities hit thermal limits. Burke solves both simultaneously. The on-campus hydrogen microgrid eliminates grid dependency; Lake Erie eliminates mechanical chiller dependency.

"Lake Erie — one of the largest surface freshwater bodies on Earth — serves as the primary feedstock for green hydrogen production, the cooling medium for a hyperscale data center, and a living demonstration of responsible water stewardship through active discharge reoxygenation."
